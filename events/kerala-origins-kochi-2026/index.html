Around the cupping table

Kerala’s coffee reaches the table through many hands. This Kochi gathering brings growers and coffee drinkers together to explore the differences between regions, coffee varieties and processing methods.

  • Taste and compare Kerala-grown coffees.
  • Meet the growers and hear about their farms.
  • Explore the processes that shape each cup.
