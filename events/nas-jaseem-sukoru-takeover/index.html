NAS × Jaseem — Sukoru Bar Takeover

Past-event archive: NAS and Jaseem at Sukoru on 8 November 2026, serving Kerala Origins coffees roasted by Bermito. An independently organised gathering during Kozhikode’s coffee weekend.

Date
8 November 2026
Time
10 AM–1 PM IST
Venue
Sukoru, English Parambil, near District Court, Kozhikode

Kerala Origins coffees, roasted by Bermito

NAS and Jaseem joined the bar at Sukoru for a morning of Kerala coffees. Kerala Origins connected the coffees with their producers, while Bermito Coffee Roasters roasted them for brewing.

SukoruBermito Coffee RoastersKerala Origins

Part of Kozhikode’s coffee weekend

This takeover was organised independently at Sukoru during the same weekend as Kerala Coffee Festival. It is recorded here separately from the festival’s official programme.
