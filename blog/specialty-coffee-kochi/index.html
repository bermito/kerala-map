Specialty coffee in Kochi: plan a thoughtful café visit

Explore the Kochi coffee directory, plan around Kaloor and Panampilly Nagar, and check the listed Third Wave Coffee branches.

Specialty Coffee Kerala Map · 3 October 2026

Choose a neighbourhood first

Kochi is easier to explore when you begin with an area rather than a long list of cafés. In this map, Kochi belongs to Ernakulam district. Open that district to find the current café, roaster and education listings, or use the full directory if you prefer a simple list.

Choose one place near your existing plans, check its own website or map listing and allow time to enjoy the coffee. A short conversation about the beans can be more useful than rushing through several stops. Opening hours, menus and access can change, so confirm details before travelling.

Kaloor: coffee and learning

The directory includes 90Million Coffee on Stadium Link Road and CAPI Specialty Coffee Roasters on Azad Road. It also lists Pour Over Lab for coffee learning. These are different kinds of visits: a café stop, a roaster visit and a class should not be treated as interchangeable.

For a roastery tasting or workshop, contact the business first. Ask whether an appointment is needed, how long the session runs and whether beginners are welcome. A listing describes a place; it does not reserve a session.

Panampilly Nagar and the Third Wave update

Cafficana’s Kochi listing places it in Panampilly Nagar. Start with the business’s current information if you want to explore manual brewing there. Availability of a particular coffee or brewing method can vary.

Third Wave Coffee now lists both Panampilly Nagar and Noel Focus Mall in its official store directory, with branch addresses and phone numbers. Panampilly Nagar lists daily hours of 8 AM to midnight. Noel Focus Mall does not publish opening hours, so call that branch before visiting. Both listings in this map now link to the official store pages and Google Maps.

Let the directory stay useful

The map is edited as information is checked. An absent café is not a judgement about every drink it serves, and an included café is not a guarantee of a particular experience. If you find an incorrect address or can supply an official opening announcement, send a correction through Add a Place in the About panel.

Before leaving, save the business’s own contact details, decide what you would like to try and check whether your visit requires a booking. The map helps you find the next conversation; the people at the café can tell you what is brewing today.
