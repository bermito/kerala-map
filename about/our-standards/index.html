About this directory

A map you can trust.

A useful connection to specialty coffee

We look for identifiable coffee origins, roasting or processing work, considered brewing, coffee education, or a relevant role in the local coffee community. A listing is an editorial selection, not a score or certification.

Sources over assumptions

Official business information, producer accounts and first-hand corrections guide the directory. Unconfirmed visiting details are labelled. Missing listings mean we have not verified an entry yet.

One place, one count

A café can sell beans and a farm can process coffee. They can appear in multiple filters while contributing once to the total. City aliases share the same records.

Connections with context

Stockists, roasted lots and event participation are different relationships. We label them separately, link sources where available and never treat an event connection as a permanent supply agreement.
