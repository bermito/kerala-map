Kerala coffee, and how this map works

What is Specialty Coffee Kerala Map?

Specialty Coffee Kerala Map is an independent map and discovery platform for Kerala's coffee community. It helps people discover specialty cafés, coffee roasters, farms, roasted coffee, coffee equipment, education and events across the state.

What is specialty coffee?

Specialty coffee is recognised for distinctive attributes such as flavour, origin, processing and the people behind it. Its value is not defined by a price or a single score alone.

Does Kerala produce coffee?

Yes. Kerala is one of India's major coffee-producing states. Coffee is mainly cultivated in small farms, with Robusta being the most widely grown variety.

Where is coffee grown in Kerala?

Kerala's main coffee-growing regions include Wayanad, Idukki and Nelliyampathy in Palakkad. Wayanad is the largest coffee-growing region in the state.

Which types of coffee are grown in Kerala?

Robusta is the most widely grown coffee in Kerala, followed by smaller quantities of Arabica. Liberica and Excelsa are also cultivated in limited quantities in certain parts of the state.

What is special about Wayanad Robusta coffee?

Wayanad is known for Robusta grown in shaded landscapes. Flavour varies with variety, processing, roast and brewing; a regional name alone does not promise a particular taste.

How can I find specialty coffee shops near me in Kerala?

Select your district on the Kerala Specialty Coffee Map to explore listed cafés and other coffee-related places. Open a listing to access its available website, Google Maps location or Instagram profile.

Does the platform include only cafés?

No. The platform includes specialty cafés, coffee roasters, farms and producers, roasted coffee brands, specialty coffee equipment businesses, coffee education, workshops and events across Kerala.

How are places selected for the map?

We review available information such as official websites, social-media profiles, menus, photographs, location details and other evidence related to specialty coffee. Information can change, so visitors should confirm directly with the business before travelling.

Is a listing a certification or recommendation?

No. Specialty Coffee Kerala is a discovery directory, not a ranking or certification system. Inclusion does not represent an endorsement, award or guarantee of quality.

How can I add a coffee-related place or business?

Use the “Add a Place” option to submit a café, roastery, farm, producer, roasted coffee brand, coffee equipment business or coffee education centre. Every submission will be reviewed before it is added.

How can I correct information on an existing listing?

Submit the updated details through the “Add a Place” form and clearly mention that it is a correction. Include an official website, Google Maps link or Instagram profile wherever possible.

Can I visit the coffee farms shown on the map?

Some farms may welcome visitors, while others are working farms without public access. Check the individual listing and contact the farm directly before planning your visit.

Where can I find coffee events and workshops in Kerala?

Visit the Events section to discover upcoming coffee workshops, tastings, competitions, festivals and community gatherings in Kerala.

Can I purchase roasted coffee or equipment through this website?

Specialty Coffee Kerala is a discovery platform, not an online shop. Roasted coffee and equipment listings may direct you to the seller's official website, Google Maps page or Instagram profile, where you can contact or purchase from them directly.
